Hardware references

  • Waveshare — shared 2.1 / 2.1B documentation and J9, J1 and power schematic. The rear PCB photo is from the 2.1 product page; the manufacturer publishes a shared schematic. Check your board revision before soldering.
  • Adafruit #3421 — pinout and Knowles — 100 kΩ DATA pull-down. The microphone photo shows the sound-port side with pin labels.
  • DFRobot DFR0954 — module, pins and 3.3–5 V supply. The manufacturer rendering is rotated 90°, not mirrored. Verify Waveshare VCC with a low battery too; operation over the full battery range has not been validated.
  • Akyga AKY0107 datasheet: maximum charge and discharge current 1C = 1.35 A; standard charge 0.2C, fast charge 0.5C. These are battery ratings, not measurements of the Waveshare charger. Its published schematic suggests an approximately 2 A setting; adjust and measure before charging this pack.
  • Kamami — Akyga product photo and speaker 560816 photo.
